Local-first logging and Garmin as the system of record

Confirming an entry only appends it to a local outbox file. Nothing waits for the network. The Today screen rebuilds from the outbox at once, and delivery to Garmin happens afterwards in a drain. After a drain the app re-reads the day from Garmin, because Garmin is authoritative.

Outboxes

  • Three outboxes: food, weight and water. Each process has its own file (Application Support/GarminKit/outbox-<process>.json); the client-generated UUID is the idempotency key.
  • Entry states: pending, sent, failed, and created, awaiting delete (an edit: Garmin has no edit route, so the corrected entry is created first and the old one deleted).
  • Retries: up to 5 attempts, exponential backoff with full jitter from 0.5 s, capped at 8 s. A 429 honours Retry-After and ends the drain cycle. Being offline or signed out never uses up an attempt. After 5 failures the entry is failed and waits for you in the Sync queue.
  • An edit whose delete keeps failing is “parked” (never marked failed). A delete that returns 404 counts as done.
  • Drains run on foreground, after every log, on “Sync now”, and in a background refresh task (com.mlcousek.garminfood.refresh, earliest 30 min after scheduling; whether iOS runs it for a sideloaded app is unverified).

Reconciliation

Entries are matched on date, meal, food id, serving id and amount. An entry logged up to 10 minutes before a delivery can count toward it. A duplicate is deleted only when it is provably the app's own re-send (timestamp equal to a local entry within 0.002 s); anything else is kept.

Sign-in

An in-app web view catches the Garmin SSO ticket and exchanges it for OAuth1 (about a year) and OAuth2 (about 24 h, refreshed with less than 300 s left). Tokens live in this process's Keychain, AfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly, so they never go into device backups. Only an expired long-lived token flips the app to “needs sign-in”; that shows the auth banner (“Garmin sign-in expired… Entries still save locally”). A separate delivery banner says how many entries haven't reached Garmin.

Auth failures are loud; everything else degrades quietly. Any failed delivery surfaces in the Sync queue and in Diagnostics.

Garmin mode and standalone mode

The data mode is Garmin or Standalone (dataMode.v1). At launch, an install that has a Garmin token or any local history is silently Garmin mode; a fresh install goes through onboarding (“With my Garmin account” / “Just on this phone”). A hidden Diagnostics switch, “Force standalone mode (testing)”, overrides the stored mode.

What standalone mode changes

  • No Garmin calls at all: no auth refresh, drains, reconciliation, health or profile refresh, gamification signal reads or background delivery.
  • Food lives in a local log, one JSON file per month (FoodLog/2026-09.json), with nutrients snapshotted at confirm time.
  • Goals come from your own nutrition plan (Settings → Nutrition plan, with the goal calculator). Water defaults to 2000 ml.
  • Search uses your foods, the offline Czech index and Open Food Facts, never Garmin. Barcodes check your custom foods first.
  • Gamification that needs Garmin activity data is hidden, not shown locked: activity challenges, bingo squares and journeys are never offered, and Garmin-only badges disappear unless you already earned them. Use the Standalone only filter to see what's left.

Switching (Settings → Data)

  • Garmin → standalone: refused while a drain runs. Undelivered food entries can be delivered first or kept on the phone (converted to local entries). The Garmin token is kept; Garmin history is not copied by the switch.
  • Standalone → Garmin: completes only after a successful sign-in. The local log stays on the phone and is not uploaded.
  • “Copy my last 90 days from Garmin…” (standalone): reads 90 days, one day at a time, oldest first, never writes. Re-running skips what's already copied. Stops on a sign-in problem, a rate limit, or 5 failed days in a row.

Data safety and backups

Unreadable files are quarantined, never overwritten

Every JSON store loads through one routine. A missing file is empty. A file that can't be read yet (phone locked after reboot) is left alone and saves are refused. A file that doesn't decode is moved aside to <name>.unreadable-<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss>.json and logged as an error.

Backups on this phone

  • An automatic snapshot once a day on foreground; “Back up now” for a manual one. The newest 14 automatic/manual snapshots are kept, plus the newest 5 safety snapshots taken right before any restore. A failed automatic attempt waits 1 h.
  • Excluded: the diagnostics log, the Garmin health cache, the offline index, anything whose path mentions outbox, token, oauth, cookie, credential or password, and any file containing token-like keys. Undelivered outbox entries are therefore never in a backup.
  • Preferences travel as typed JSON, except dataSafety.*, developer.* and system keys.

Export, import, restore

  • Export writes one file, GarminFood-backup-<yyyy-MM-dd>.json (schema garminfood.backup, format 1).
  • Import shows a preview (date, app version, counts of food entries, custom foods, meals, favorites, weigh-ins, drinks, day notes, fasts, supplements, progress files) before anything changes. Newer formats or store versions are refused.
  • A restore is staged and applied at the next launch, before any store loads, after a safety snapshot.
  • Today shows a backup reminder when you haven't exported for 14 days and didn't dismiss it in the last 14 days.

Source: GarminKit/PersistedJSON.swift, FoodLogCore/Backup/*, GarminFood/App/DataSafetyLaunch.swift, Profile/DataSettingsView.swift, Today/Slots/BackupReminderBanner.swift

Where results come from

  • Your foods (custom, favorites, foods in your history) and the offline Czech index answer on every keystroke.
  • Garmin (Garmin mode) and Open Food Facts are asked after a 250 ms pause and only from 2 characters. Pages are cached (100 terms, 15 min). Garmin pages are 50 items, region CZ, with “Show more from Garmin”.
  • Open Food Facts searches Czech products only by default (“Search Czech foods only”); an empty Czech result retries worldwide once. Each OFF product becomes one 100 g serving.
  • Up to two spellings are sent: what you typed, plus a copy with diacritics removed or restored from a Czech food vocabulary.

Ranking

Text is normalized (diacritics stripped, numbers glued to units, light Czech stemming so all kuře/kuřecí forms meet). Each query word takes its best match: exact 1.0, stem 0.9, prefix 0.8, stem-prefix 0.7, one or two typos 0.6, first-letter typo 0.5. Brand matches count half. Anything under 0.45 is dropped. Bonuses: +0.15 when every word matched, +0.10 when the first words agree; a personal boost from how often you log it, +0.15 for a favorite, +0.10 for a custom food. Duplicates across sources merge when id or EAN match, or name, brand and kcal/100 g (within 5%) agree; the row then says “also in …”.

Offline Czech index

A gzipped index of Czech Open Food Facts products, built weekly in CI and published as a GitHub release. The app checks the manifest at most once every 24 h, on Wi-Fi unless “Allow on cellular” is on, and swaps the file only after verifying its SHA-256. “Download now” in Settings skips the throttle. It returns at most 50 candidates and needs a word of 2+ letters.

Barcodes

  • EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-E, Code 128, ITF-14, GS1 DataBar; the whole camera frame is scanned. Manual entry takes 8–14 digits.
  • Garmin mode: Garmin's barcode search (then without a leading zero for 13-digit codes), then the offline index. Nothing found opens the custom-food editor with the code noted.
  • Standalone: your custom foods' barcodes, the offline index, then Open Food Facts; nothing found opens the custom-food editor with the barcode filled in.

Open Food Facts product → Garmin food

In Garmin mode an OFF product is matched to a Garmin food first: the name (without brand and pack size) must match and calories agree within 20%. You always see the result before logging (“Use this match”, “That's not right”, or pick another). With no match, “Create in Garmin” is an explicit, experimental write with a warning.

Confirm, edit, move, duplicate, copy

Confirm

Food and brand, serving, amount (grams or servings, Czech decimal comma accepted), live kcal preview, nutrients per serving, then meal and date. “Log it” turns into “Logged!” and closes after 0.5 s. The amount must be above 0 and at most 10,000 servings.

Default meal: the meal card you came from; otherwise Garmin's meal windows (“Default meal from Garmin's schedule”, on by default; outside every window = Snacks); otherwise by clock:

Row actions

  • Edit changes amount and meal only. A synced entry is replaced (new entry first, then the old one is deleted, so both can show briefly). Edits earn no XP.
  • Move to is an edit with a new meal. Duplicate logs the same food again and counts like any log.
  • Delete of a synced entry calls Garmin directly (“It will also be removed from Garmin Connect.”); a queued one is removed from the phone, unless it is being sent right now.
  • Quick-add entries (no food id) can only be deleted.

Copy from…

Pick any past day and meal (defaults to yesterday's same meal); every copyable item starts ticked. Items are logged one by one into the meal you're viewing; if one fails, the earlier ones stay logged.

Sync queue

Settings → Sync queue lists everything Garmin hasn't accepted, including weight and water: “Waiting to sync”, “Sent, confirming…”, “Failed after N attempts”, “Edited, removing the old entry…”. Retry, delete or “Sync now” from here; it is the only place a failed entry becomes active again.

Meal presets, custom foods, favorites, day notes

Meal presets

A named list of foods with amounts and an optional note, built with “New meal”. Logging asks for Portions (scales every ingredient), meal and date, and creates one entry per ingredient. “Sync to Garmin (experimental)” creates a Garmin custom meal on an unconfirmed route; only offered in Garmin mode for presets without custom foods.

Custom foods

Name, brand, serving, calories, carbs, protein, fat, sugar, sodium and a note. In Garmin mode you must pick the closest Garmin food and a multiplier, because that is what Garmin records (the confirm screen says “Recorded in Garmin as …”). In standalone mode calories are required and a barcode can be saved.

Favorites

Local only (Garmin's favorites route is unconfirmed). Star on any search row or card; favorites add +0.15 to search ranking.

Day notes

One note per day with free text and tags: 🏃 Race, 🏋️ Training, 🎉 Celebration, 🤒 Sick, ✈️ Travel, 🍺 Party, 😴 Rest day. Autosaves about 600 ms after typing stops. Tagged days show as emoji on Trends charts, and the Race tag drives the race-day sport badges.

Source: FoodLogCore/MealPreset.swift, CustomFood.swift, FavoriteFood.swift, DayNote.swift

Weight, water and fasting

Weight

Entries between 0 and 500 kg, optional past date and note. History merges Garmin weigh-ins with unsynced local ones; the chart needs 2 entries and shows the last 90. The goal uses your override or Garmin's target and start weight; “reached” is within 0.05 kg. The ETA comes from a least-squares trend over 14 days (4+ weigh-ins), else Garmin's planned rate, and is shown only when heading the right way and within 3 years.

Water

Quick add 100 / 250 / 500 ml or a custom amount up to 5000 ml. Today's total is Garmin's day total plus drinks not yet delivered. Goal: your override, Garmin's goal, else 2000 ml. Overrides are never written to Garmin.

Fasting

Off by default; default window 20:00 → 12:00, repeating daily. A fast is named by the day it ends and is broken by the first food logged inside the window (logs back-filled to other days don't count). Confirm screens show a gentle “You're fasting until …” and never block. History covers 30 days with “Days kept in a row” and “Kept, last 30 days”.

Source: WeightTracking.swift, WeightGoalProgress.swift, HydrationTracking.swift, FastingSchedule.swift, FastingLogMoments.swift

Goals and the goal calculator

In Garmin mode targets come from Garmin and Settings shows them read-only. In standalone mode your plan is stored with a start date: a saved plan applies from today and earlier days keep their targets.

“Goal met” for gamification: calories inside the green band (95–105%), protein, carbs and fat each at least their goal.

Calculator (standalone)

  • BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) = 10·kg + 6.25·cm − 5·age, +5 (male) or −161 (female).
  • TDEE = BMR × activity: Mostly sitting 1.2 · Lightly active 1.375 · Moderately active 1.55 · Very active 1.725 · Extremely active 1.9.
  • Target = TDEE ∓ pace × 7700 / 7 for losing or gaining; pace 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 kg a week and never more than 1% of body weight. The target never goes below max(1200 kcal, BMR).
  • Protein 1.6 g/kg when losing, else 1.4 g/kg; fat 30% of kcal (25% if protein + fat would exceed the target); carbs take the rest.
  • Valid inputs: age 14–100, height 100–250 cm, weight 30–300 kg. The calculator saves nothing until “Use these numbers”.

Part 6

Glossary

Terms used in the app and this guide
Nutrition day
The date an entry is logged for (local midnight to midnight). Streaks, XP and goals all use it, so they agree with Garmin even for a 01:00 log.
Outbox
The local file where a confirmed entry waits until Garmin accepts it.
Drain
One pass that sends waiting outbox entries to Garmin.
Reconciliation
Re-reading the day from Garmin after a drain and matching it with local entries.
Garmin mode / Standalone
Whether Garmin is the system of record, or everything stays on the phone.
Grace day
The one missed day per rolling 7 days that doesn't break the food streak.
Streak freeze
An automatic save of a streak (3+ days) from a miss in the last 7 days. Earned from a full bingo card or a boss defeat; hold at most 2.
XP, level, tier
Experience points; a level is a deterministic function of total XP; a tier is the named band of levels (Newcomer … Legend).
Rarity
Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Core badges derive it from their condition; feature badges state it.
Challenge window
Days a long-running challenge has before it rotates out uncompleted (no XP).
Rotation weight
How likely a template is to be picked next: creative 3, hand-authored 2, two ladder tiers per family 1, everything else 0.
Signals
Per-day facts built from your log: food tags (fish, fruit, colours, cuisines, Czech dishes and brands…), macros, water, activities, weight, meal times.
Data requirement
What a rule needs (macros, water, activities). A rule is offered only when one of your last 14 days has that data.
Bingo line
A row, column or diagonal of the 3×3 weekly card; the centre is free.
Boss target
How many good days of its habit beat this week's boss (3–7).
Journey
A long cumulative tally turned into a trip: protein → metres climbed, water → litres, active kcal → km, foods → passport stamps.
PR
Personal record: a strict improvement on your best day for one of 8 measures.
Collection
A set of dishes or brands you discover by logging a food with the matching tag.
Limited edition
A seasonal event badge, earned only inside the event's window.
Svátek
Czech name day; the owner's (Jiří, 24 April) becomes a personal seasonal event.
Optional source
A feature outside the level curve (supplements). Its XP is scaled so all optional sources add at most 0.5% of the core pace.
UL
Tolerable upper intake level (EFSA). “Safe level” is a softer EFSA figure (iron, caffeine).
Label score
A 0–100 score of a supplement's label: transparency 40, dose 40, headroom 20.
